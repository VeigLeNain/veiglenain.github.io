Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains how Pull of Curiosity Uploader (the "App") handles data. The App is a private tool run by the owner of the Pull of Curiosity YouTube channel on their own computer. It is not offered to the public.

What the App does

The App uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload and schedule videos on the Pull of Curiosity channel, with their title, description and tags;
  • read basic information about that channel and its videos (title, video list, view and like counts);
  • read YouTube Analytics reports for that channel (views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers gained).

Data accessed and how it is used

The App only accesses the YouTube account that explicitly authorises it through Google's sign-in screen. The data above is used solely to publish the channel's own videos and to measure their performance so future videos can be improved. The App does not access data about viewers beyond the aggregated, anonymous statistics that YouTube Analytics provides.

Storage

The OAuth access token and the statistics are stored only on the channel owner's computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the App, and no database of users is kept.

Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is exchanged only with Google/YouTube, as required to use the YouTube API Services.

Google and YouTube

By using the App you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the App's access at any time from your Google Account security settings: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Locally stored tokens and statistics are deleted when access is revoked or on request, and in any case are not kept longer than needed to operate the channel.
